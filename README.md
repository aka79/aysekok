## Research Overview

My research explores how we can make advanced AI systems **more understandable, evaluable, governable, and accountable** as they move from research environments into real-world institutions.

I work across the intersection of **frontier-model behavior, AI assurance, documentation, and governance**. A central focus of my work is connecting what we can observe and measure in AI systems—such as behavioral risks, model capabilities, failure modes, and robustness—to the institutional processes needed to manage those risks responsibly.

My research draws on and contributes to emerging work in **AI evaluation and assurance, NIST and IEEE frameworks, international AI governance, and public-interest technology**. I am particularly interested in the space between technical research and institutional practice: how evidence about AI systems can become useful documentation, evaluation protocols, governance mechanisms, and standards.

> 💡 *My goal is to help build an AI governance ecosystem in which advanced systems are not only more capable, but also more measurable, transparent, accountable, and worthy of human trust.*

---

## Pillars of Inquiry

- 🛡️ **AI Evaluation & Assurance**  
  Studying methods for evaluating advanced AI systems, including behavioral testing, capability and risk assessment, robustness, interpretability, and failure-mode analysis.

- 🧠 **Frontier AI Behavior & Alignment**  
  Investigating how increasingly capable models behave under different conditions, including questions of alignment, agency, tool use, model oversight, and the reliability of existing mechanisms.

- 🏛️ **AI Governance, Standards & Institutional Design**  
  Exploring how technical evidence can be translated into practical governance through frameworks such as the **NIST AI Risk Management Framework**, **IEEE AI standards**, and emerging international approaches to AI assurance.

- 📚 **Documentation & AI Accountability**  
  Examining documentation as a core component of AI governance—from model and system documentation to evaluation records, provenance, audit trails, and evidence needed for meaningful oversight.

- 🌐 **Human-Centered & Public-Interest AI**  
  Studying the broader institutional and societal implications of AI deployment, with particular attention to human agency, education, trustworthy systems, and accountable use of AI in public-interest settings.

---

## Research Question

> **How can society develop the technical evidence, institutional capacity, and governance infrastructure needed to evaluate and responsibly deploy increasingly capable AI systems?**
